# The Viral Playbook — deep strategy reference

Read this when planning content, writing captions, or explaining strategy to
the user. This is the "why" behind every move the toolkit makes.

## 1. How the Instagram algorithm actually ranks content (2026)

Instagram ranks by **predicted user satisfaction**, measured in signals:
1. **Watch time / completion rate** (reels) — the #1 signal. Looping videos that
   get rewatched outperform everything.
2. **Sends per reach** — shares to DMs. Content people *send* ("look at this")
   gets pushed to non-followers. Design posts that tag a friend's situation.
3. **Comments** — questions, mild debate, "comment X for Y" mechanics.
4. **Saves** — utility content (recipes, tutorials, checklists).
5. **Likes** — weakest signal, still a floor.

Non-follower reach comes from: Explore page, Reels tab, suggested posts, and
the audio page. Using a **trending audio early** in its cycle is the single
cheapest reach hack.

## 2. Formats ranked by growth efficiency

| Format | Best for | Watch-time trick |
|---|---|---|
| Reels 7–15s | memes, POVs, quick hacks | loop end→start seamlessly |
| Reels 20–40s | tutorials, storytelling | open loop in frame 1, pay off at 80% |
| Carousels 6–10 slides | tips, before/after, guides | slide 1 = bold claim, slide 2 = "swipe if..." |
| Stories | retention, polls, DMs | 3–5 frames, poll in frame 2 |
| Photo posts | memes, fits, aesthetic | strong first image only |

## 3. The Hook Bank (first 1.5 seconds / first line)

Formulas that reliably stop the scroll:
- **POV:** "POV: your boss replies 'ok' to your 4-paragraph message"
- **Callout:** "If you're a college student with ₹2000, this is for you"
- **Negation:** "Stop doing X" / "You're using Y wrong"
- **Secret:** "Nobody tells beginners this" / "The trick they gatekeep"
- **Curiosity gap:** "I found the cheapest paradise in India (it's not Goa)"
- **Challenge:** "I did this for 30 days and..."
- **List:** "3 mistakes killing your ..."

Rules: ≤90 characters, no warm-up words ("hey guys", "so today"), text overlay
on the FIRST frame, visual motion in frame 1.

## 4. Caption structure (H-T-P-C)

**Hook** (stop scroll) → **Tension** (keep reading: story, stakes, relatability)
→ **Payoff** (value delivered) → **CTA** (one question or action, max one).

First line ≤125 chars (before "...more" cutoff). Comment your own post with
extra value (recipe, links, part-2 teaser) — pinned comments lift engagement.

## 5. Hashtag strategy

8–15 tags, 3 tiers: big (1M+, reach lottery), mid (100k–1M, sweet spot),
niche (<100k, rankable). Rotate 2–3 sets (the scheduler/templates rotate for
you). Never: banned tags, #follow4follow, repeating identical sets daily.

## 6. Niche cheatsheets

- **Fitness**: transformations > tips. "Day X vs Day Y" + trending audio.
  Post 6–8am IST (pre-workout scroll) and 8–10pm.
- **Food**: hook with the final dish FIRST, then process. 12–20s. Post 12–1pm
  and 7–9pm (hungry hours). Recipe in comments = comment farming.
- **Travel**: destination reveal at second 2. "₹X trip" price-anchor hooks.
  Post Thu–Sat evenings for weekend planning.
- **Fashion**: transitions (snap, twirl) on beat. GRWM format. Post 7–9pm.
- **Tech**: screen-record demos, text-overlay heavy. "free alternative" angle.
- **Comedy**: POV + regional specificity wins. Post 9–11pm IST.

## 7. Weekly cadence that works

- 1 reel/day minimum (7/week) — reels are the growth engine
- 2 carousels/week (Tue/Thu) — saves + authority
- Stories daily (2–4 frames) — retention + poll data
- 1 community week/month: reply-to-comment reels, Q&As

## 8. The First-Hour Protocol

First 60 min decide distribution. Post, then:
1. Reply to every comment (drafts need user approval for DM-touching tools)
2. Share reel to story with "new drop" sticker
3. Send to 2–3 close friends who engage (ask user which friends, never DM
   without consent)
4. Do NOT edit caption/hashtags in the first hour (resets indexing)

## 9. What gets you shadowbanned (teach the user)

- Posting >5x/day from one account/session
- Banned hashtags, engagement-bait language violations
- Mass following/unfollowing (bots)
- Re-uploading watermarked TikTok content
- Music with copyright claims on business accounts
The toolkit's caps and delays exist for exactly these reasons.
